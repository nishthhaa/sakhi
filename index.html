Know this

Zero FIR — you can file an FIR at any police station, not only the one where the incident happened. They cannot refuse it.

Night arrest rule — a woman cannot be arrested after sunset or before sunrise except with a magistrate's written permission, and only by a woman officer.

Free legal aid — the District Legal Services Authority provides a lawyer at no cost. Call 15100.

Your statement — you can ask to give your statement at home, in the presence of a woman officer or a relative.
